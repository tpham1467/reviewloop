# Governance

reviewloop is maintained by its owner, GitHub user `tpham1467` (the maintainer).

- The maintainer makes final decisions on scope, releases and merges.
- Changes land through pull requests with one approval and green CI. Squash merge only.
- Larger design changes are discussed in an issue or an ADR under `docs/adr/` before implementation.
- Contributors who show sustained, high-quality work may be invited as maintainers by the current maintainer.
- Conduct issues follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
